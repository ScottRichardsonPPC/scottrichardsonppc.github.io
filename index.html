    Tracks come from Scott's GPS logs and are simplified, so times, distances and heights are approximate. Height is above the ground where he took off, not exactly above your spot. Some flights were never GPS-tracked.

    Purple dots are photos Scott posted on Facebook, placed by the camera's GPS or the location stamped on the photo. Photo links open on Facebook, which may ask you to log in. Gold dots (turn on “Best-of photos” at top right) are Scott’s favorite photos from his photo library, placed where the aircraft was when each was taken; the subjects listed with them are guesses.

    A video linked to a flight may not include every part of that flight. See also the flight history for yearly totals, performance, photos and exposure stats.
